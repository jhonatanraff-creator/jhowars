# Intro

“Poster 2024” (also known as “Poster Vol 1”) is a curated series of handcrafted prints, created using traditional manual techniques such as offset press and screen printing. This collection explores the tactile beauty and raw authenticity of printmaking, transforming each poster into a unique piece of art.

# Section: Manual Printmaking in an Independent Studio

Each poster was meticulously crafted in an independent print studio, merging offset and screen printing to achieve rich textures and vivid colors. The manual process brings a unique character to every print, emphasizing the artistry of traditional methods in a modern context.

# Section: Design and Production: Jhonatan Rafael

From concept to production, Jhonatan Rafael oversaw every detail of this project. “Poster 2024” is a celebration of manual labor in the digital age, reflecting a dedication to the timeless craft of printmaking.
