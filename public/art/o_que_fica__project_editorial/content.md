# Intro

“O Que Fica” is an editorial project that embarks on a journey of visual and conceptual exploration. This A4 format book merges form, color, and idea, transforming it into a true art object. It’s a bold dive into graphic and editorial experimentation, pushing boundaries beyond traditional content.

# Section: Graphic and Editorial Innovations

This project redefines editorial creation, using graphic design to narrate a unique visual story. Each page is crafted to provoke thought and inspire, blending text and imagery to offer new perspectives on visual storytelling.

# Section: Text and Design: Jhonatan Rafael and Dalton Albertin

Jhonatan Rafael and Dalton Albertin collaborated on this project, combining their design and narrative expertise. “O Que Fica” is a result of creative synergy, translating the essence of what remains into every detail, creating a lasting impact in graphic and editorial design.
